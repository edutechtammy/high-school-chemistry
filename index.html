Adobe Captivate PLC Examples and Ideas for Chemistry

The goal of sharing the content here about what I have built for my own high school chemistry course that I taught for 15 years is to have a springboard for what can be built for the TSTC courses. There are a lot of resources here including interactive lessons, stand alone interactive activities, quiz bank questions, lab report starters, a lab report grading rubric that can be applied to every lab, and Chem-mysteries which are story-based scenarios, usually a medical or crime mystery, where students cain the module and put it to use in a simulated real world application.
