Getting Font Fancy:

Use a Google Font:

A link to Google fonts is included in the head section with a specific font selected, Tangerine. This lets you use the Tangerine font at https://fonts.google.com/ by adding it in the head tag and inline in a style in a tag like this ...

Tangerine

or even add an effect such as a shadow which will be there when viewed on the web...

Tangerine

You can select any Google font you want, just change it from Tangerine in the head section and inline to the name of the font you want. Note that if you select a font with a space in the name, replace it with a plus sign in all locations. Since these are online accessed fonts, remember they will not be available if the student views them offline. This is why we add the comma and the word serif after the Google font. It is a fallback font.
